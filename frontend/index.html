Privacy By Architecture

Your Documents Never Leave Your Computer

PDF Swiss-Knife runs 100% locally on your machine. No telemetry, no cloud relays, no file size limits, and no subscriptions.

Complete Privacy

Sensitive legal, financial, and confidential documents are processed exclusively in your machine's memory and local disk.

Fully Offline

No internet connection required. Work seamlessly in air-gapped environments, on flights, or during remote deployments.

No File Caps

Process documents with hundreds of pages or gigabytes in size without server upload timeouts or arbitrary size caps.
